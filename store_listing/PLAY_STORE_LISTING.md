## Full description

```
M6 Sudoku — Think. Solve. Master.
Challenge your mind with M6 Sudoku, a modern Sudoku experience designed for both casual players and dedicated puzzle enthusiasts.
Whether you have a few minutes to spare or want to complete a challenging puzzle, M6 Sudoku gives you a clean, focused and enjoyable way to play Sudoku wherever you are.

Classic Sudoku Gameplay
Solve traditional 9×9 Sudoku puzzles by filling every row, column and 3×3 box with the numbers 1–9.
Choose your preferred difficulty and work your way from easier puzzles to more challenging games as your skills improve.

Multiple Difficulty Levels
Choose the challenge that suits you:
* Easy
* Medium
* Hard
* Expert
Each difficulty provides a different level of challenge, giving you room to improve at your own pace.

Notes & Smart Gameplay
Use notes to keep track of possible numbers while solving difficult cells.
Switch between Numbers and Notes whenever you need to plan your next move.

Helpful Hints
Stuck on a puzzle?
Use a hint when you need a little assistance and keep your game moving without giving up.

Track Your Progress
Keep an eye on your solving time, mistakes and hints used.
Improve your speed and accuracy as you play more puzzles.

Daily Challenges
Take on a daily Sudoku challenge and test yourself with a fresh puzzle each day.
Build consistency and make Sudoku part of your daily routine.

Achievements & Statistics
Track your Sudoku journey with detailed statistics and achievements.
See how you're progressing and challenge yourself to improve your best times and solving performance.

Designed for Focus
M6 Sudoku features a clean, modern interface designed to keep the focus on the puzzle.
With an easy-to-use number pad, notes mode, undo, erase and hint controls, everything you need is within easy reach.

Exercise Your Mind
Sudoku is more than just filling numbers. It encourages logical thinking, pattern recognition, concentration and problem-solving.
Take a break, challenge yourself and solve a puzzle.

Download M6 Sudoku and start solving.
```
